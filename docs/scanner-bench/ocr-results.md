# Scanner benchmark: OCR of the prepared images

Generated by `docs/ocr-bench/score.py --images` from `run.sh --images` (method: docs/scanner-bench/README.md). Every image was recognised exactly as prepared on the PC: PSM 3, LSTM, `user_defined_dpi` from images.tsv, no detection, scaling or OSD. Variants `ours-*` and `oss-*` are the colour filters; `ossocr-app` is OSS-DocumentScanner's OCR input as its app sends it (adaptive threshold, whole page), `ossocr-blocks` the same with the header-default text-block contours, blocks stitched in their order.
