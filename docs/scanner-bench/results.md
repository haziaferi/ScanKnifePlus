## 3. Colour filters

Every labelled photo warped with its hand-marked quad by our PerspectiveCrop (corpus pages to A4 at 300 DPI, others to their natural size capped at 3508 px), so detection plays no part. Ours run on the JVM, theirs (default options, as the app's transform list applies them) in Python/OpenCV. L* is CIELAB lightness (0-100). Paper = pixels above the unfiltered page's Otsu threshold, eroded 5x5; ink = the rest (not eroded, so thin strokes count). Uniformity: standard deviation and p5-p95 spread of paper L* (lower is flatter). Contrast: median paper L* minus median ink L* (higher is crisper); stroke cores: median paper L* minus the 10th percentile of ink L*, which still sees text a filter has thinned. Medians over pages; PC ms is median time per page, not device time.

## 5. Auto-capture

Synthetic detections at 30 fps for 6 s, 20 seeded runs per scenario. A page fills about 60% x 70% of a portrait frame with a slight perspective; every frame adds Gaussian corner noise of 0.2% of the frame (detector jitter). Ours gets the quads in normalised coordinates, as LiveScanController feeds AutoCaptureDetector (without QuadSmoother in between), and calls notifyCaptured after each capture. Theirs gets the same quads in analysis-frame pixels, quantised like their 200 px live detection; it captures at most once per document until the document is lost. Times are from the scenario start (from the stop for the sliding page); `captures per run` counts every capture in 6 s. `Should capture`: yes = a capture is wanted, no = any capture is a false capture, - = either is acceptable.
