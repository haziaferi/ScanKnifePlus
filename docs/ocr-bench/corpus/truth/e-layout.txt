Annual Report on Reading Habits

Summary for the board, October 2026

Visitors

Visits rose for the third year in a row. Most of
the growth came from weekday afternoons,
when school groups use the study rooms.

Saturday mornings remain the busiest time,
with queues at the desk before ten o'clock.

Collections

The library bought 1,204 new titles and
withdrew 830 damaged ones. Children's books
and travel guides were borrowed most often.

The scanning project added 4,316 pages to the
online archive.
